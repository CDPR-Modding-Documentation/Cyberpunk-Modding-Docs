# NPV - V as custom NPC

## Summary

Created:: 11 October 2026 by [djchromatoze](https://app.gitbook.com/u/fCd8A2nVhIZBzmuai7K846oABlI3 "mention")

As of October 2026, the previous method of creating an NPV has been completely overhauled by [MaximiliumM](https://www.nexusmods.com/profile/MaximiliumM?gameId=3333), author of Appearance Creator Mod, and Appearance Menu Mod. You are now able to create your custom NPV entirely in game, including any appearance mods, cosmetics and body textures, with no Blender or Wolvenkit required!

For a documentation of the previous process, check [npv-v-as-custom-npc](npv-v-as-custom-npc/ "mention")

### **What you will need:**

• [Appearance Creator Mod](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/10795) 2.71+ - To create, save, and share your NPV and outfits

• [Appearance Menu Mod](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/790) 2.17.0+ - To switch your NPV's outfits, or use installed pose packs.

• [Appearance Runtime Plugin](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/35126) 1.9.0+ - Required for both to work&#x20;

• [Photomode-EX](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/18839) - If you wish for your NPV to be useable in Photomode

• All required dependant mods - in this case, the [Core Mods](https://wiki.redmodding.org/cyberpunk-2077-modding/for-mod-creators-theory/core-mods-explained) will cover you

• About 5 minutes (Note: the first time you open ACM it will need to index your mods. This may take an extra 5 minutes)

### Step-by-step guide:&#x20;

1. Start your game, and open Photomode&#x20;
2. Open Appearance Creator Mod
3. Target your V (this will be displayed in the Editor tab)
4. Go to NPV tab, and give your NPV a unique name
5. Name your current outfit in the Look Name box and click "Save Look" Your game will freeze for a minute while it creates the NPV files, be patient and do not panic!&#x20;
6. (Optional: You can create as many new outfits as you wish at this time, or additional outfits can be added after NPV installation - process below)&#x20;
7. Create a Photomode icon (a 100x100px image of your V), save it as "icon.png" or "icon.jpg", and place in your NPV folder (`Cyberpunk 2077\bin\x64\plugins\cyber_engine_tweaks\mods\AppearanceCreatorMod\NPV\[your_NPV_name]`) or click "Capture Icon" and ACM will make one for you
8. Click "Create NPV"
9. From here, click "Install", then restart your game and your new NPV will appear in AMM and Photomode ready to use
10. (Optional) If you wish to share your NPV with others, click the "Share" button - this will create a .zip file in your NPV folder that you can share. _If you intend to share your NPV publicly, be sure you have the author's permission for any mods used!_
11. That's it! You're all done! Good job :)

### Optional: Creating and using custom outfits with ACM

This section describes the process for creating, saving and exporting custom outfits with ACM, which can be done either during the initial NPV creation or after installation.

#### Making outfits during NPV creation:

1. After creating your first look, toggle off CET and switch your outfit in game (note: this can include any custom cyberware, tattoos, body rigs, makeup, hair, head sculpts; each custom element will be baked into the outfit)
2. Name your outfit
3. Click "Save Look"

#### Making outfits after NPV installation:

1. Spawn your NPV with AMM or Photomode and target them
2. Open ACM Editor, place any desired item mesh on the NPV
3. Switch to "Appearances" tab
4. Input a unique tag and appearance name
5. Click "Export", outfit will appear in AMM and ACM whenever the NPV is targeted
