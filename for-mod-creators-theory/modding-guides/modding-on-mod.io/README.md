## Why should I upload to mod.io?

For the console players. Until somebody slops up reverse-engineered console firmware that allows installation of PC mods, they have literally no other way to access mods.

### Do they pay anything?

No. There are no donation points, no cash rewards, and unless somebody finds your contact information and throws money your way, you won't see a single enny for this.

### Doesn't sound like a good deal.

24h after the patch release, Cyberpunk had 49 mods _in total_ and _over a million downloads_. Console players are desperate for mods. They've been standing outside in the cold, pressing their noses against the cold glass of the windows on Nexus, watching us have our holiday — and they couldn't digest the protein.&#x20;

Now they can.

### OK, how annoying is it?

The process is not fun and has led to increased appreciation of the Nexus upload form. You have to [pack your mod](how-to-upload-on-mod.io.md#packing-your-mod) in a different format, and every uploaded mod goes through full quality assurance.

### What "quality assurance"?

If you upload a mod on mod.io, somebody in CDPR's QA department will install that mod on every console you enabled in the upload settings, and check that it does what it should. This takes time.&#x20;

As of Oct 24, there is a technical hang-up that **completely prevents any mod approval**. It'll hopefully be resolved soon.
